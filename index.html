The belief
01

Most financial products fail before the user ever loses money — they fail the moment a dashboard shows twelve numbers when the decision only needed one.

The work that matters isn't adding more data, charts or features. It's finding the single number, screen or sentence that actually changes what someone does next — and protecting it from everything that would clutter it.

Clarity isn't a design layer. It's the product.

Piyush RoyNew Delhi

I'm Piyush, based in New Delhi. I spend my time making investment products simpler than the industry usually allows.

At Perccent, Cybrilla and a few platforms before them, the work has always circled the same question — what does this person actually need to see to make a good decision, and what can we remove.

Outside work, I make time for long walks, old books and conversations that wander somewhere useful.

"The best interfaces disappear. The best decisions feel obvious in hindsight."

What this site collects. This site does not use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising scripts. The only personal information collected is the email address you choose to submit through the subscribe form, which is sent directly to Buttondown, a third-party newsletter provider, and used solely to send you occasional writing from Piyush.

Third parties involved. Two services are used to run this site: Netlify (hosting) and Buttondown (email newsletter delivery). Each may process standard technical data — such as IP address and browser type — as part of normal web hosting and email delivery. Piyush does not access, sell, or share this data beyond what these providers require to operate.

Your choices. Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes your address from Buttondown immediately. To request that your data be deleted sooner, or to ask any question about how it's handled, email piyush@funnelprolabs.com.

Changes. If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date above will change with it.
